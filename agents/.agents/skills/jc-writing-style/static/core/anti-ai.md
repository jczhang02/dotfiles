# Anti-AI cleanup

Use after drafting. The goal is not casual writing. The goal is to remove generic assistant residue while preserving JC's technical style.

## Delete or reduce

- Chatbot openings: `当然`, `下面是`, `希望这能帮助你`, `这是一个...`.
- Inflated meaning: `具有重要意义`, `至关重要`, `关键一步`, `深刻体现`, `彰显了`, unless evidence demands it.
- Formula slogans: `不仅...而且...`, `不仅仅是...更是...`, `从...到...` without real scale.
- Generic progress words: `赋能`, `打造`, `生态`, `闭环`, `范式转变`, `里程碑`.
- Mechanical lists of three when two or four items are more natural.
- Repeated paragraph endings like `具有重要意义` / `提供了参考` / `奠定基础`.
- Vague authorities: `研究表明`, `专家认为`, `已有工作指出` without citation or source.

## Keep when style-relevant

- Academic transitions such as `因此`, `此外`, `在此基础上`, `具体来说`, `实验结果表明` are allowed, but not every sentence.
- Technical repetitions are allowed when they stabilize terminology.
- Formal `本文`, `该方法`, `我们` are allowed in manuscript/report mode.
- Oral markers such as `首先`, `接下来`, `这里可以看到` are allowed in defense mode.

## Rhythm repair

- Break overlong Chinese sentences when they carry two jobs.
- Merge tiny academic sentences when they sound like bullet captions.
- Vary paragraph starts; do not start every paragraph with `此外` or `因此`.
- Prefer concrete method/result nouns over abstract praise.

## Final smell test

If the paragraph could be pasted into any AI-generated report without revealing JC's technical perspective, rewrite it with more concrete problem, method, or evidence.
